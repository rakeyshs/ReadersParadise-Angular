Thank you for visiting https://rp.primabi.co/ and using our mobile applications. By using our website or mobile applications, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

This policy applies to

Information We Collect
Section 01
1.1 Personal Information
  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, company information, and profile details when you create an account.
  • Contact Information: Information you provide when contacting us for support or inquiries.
  • Payment Information: Billing address and payment method details (processed securely by third-party payment processors).
1.2 Usage Data
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and unique device identifiers.
  • App Usage: Features used, time spent in app, crash reports, and performance data.
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address (precise location only with your explicit consent).
1.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies
  • Cookies: Small files stored on your device to enhance user experience and remember preferences.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics and similar services to understand user behavior.
  • Mobile SDKs: Third-party software development kits for analytics and crash reporting in mobile apps.
⚙️
How We Use Your Information
Section 02
  • Service Provision: To provide, maintain, and improve our project management software and related services.
  • Account Management: To create and manage your account, authenticate users, and provide customer support.
  • Communication: To send service-related notifications, updates, security alerts, and respond to inquiries.
  • Analytics and Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, improve our services, and develop new features.
  • Marketing: With your explicit consent, to send promotional emails and notifications about new features.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)
Section 03
  • Contract Performance: Processing necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • Legitimate Interest: For analytics, security, and service improvement.

  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable laws and regulations.
